Scope, Boundary & Technical Limitations Notice

Data hosted within HeatShield reflects research-grade ambient dry-bulb air temperature observations collected at 2.0 meters above ground surface across Solapur Municipal Corporation (SMC) administrative boundaries. This telemetry captures microclimatic shelter variance rather than Land Surface Temperature (LST) derived from satellite radiometry. Station observations do not substitute official synoptic warnings issued by the India Meteorological Department (IMD).

Spatial Extent: 178.57 km² SMC boundary · Sampling Interval: 10 minutes · Sensor Calibration Drift Index: ±0.35°C
