Scientific Integrity & Calibration Notice

All readings are subject to operational sensor calibration drift (nominal margin: ±0.3°C ambient, ±2.5% RH). Values represent microclimate sensor nodes deployed for academic heat mitigation research by Team TF-141 (MIT-VPU Solapur) and should not be used as official disaster management advisories. Automated QA routines flag readings deviating more than 3σ from contiguous spatial neighbors.
